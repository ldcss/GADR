# [ADR-003] Integration of External Video Collaboration Component
- **Status**: Accepted
- **Context**: The platform includes tele-health interventions such as tele-consultations and tele-interconsultations. These features require secure, low-latency video conferencing capabilities that must be linked directly to the clinical electronic health records and protocols generated by the system.
- **Decision**: Integrate the existing video collaboration API provided by RNP (Rede Nacional de Ensino e Pesquisa) instead of developing a custom video streaming infrastructure.
- **Considered Options**:
  - *Option 1: Develop a custom WebRTC video conferencing service.* Rejected because building and scaling a video streaming infrastructure is a massive duplication of effort and falls outside the core clinical domain of the project.
  - *Option 2: Commercial off-the-shelf solutions (e.g., Zoom/Meet APIs).* Deprioritized in favor of leveraging the RNP infrastructure, which is already validated and tailored for the Brazilian public education and research network.
- **Consequences**:
  - *Pros:* Drastically reduces development time, infrastructure costs, and maintenance burden by utilizing a stable, pre-validated component.
  - *Cons:* Introduces a hard external dependency on the RNP service; requires custom integration layers to synchronize video session metadata with the platform's internal clinical records.
